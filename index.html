Church · previs storyboard
3D previs from the shot list in the director's bible. White figures without faces are the six dancers of the congregation; the dark figure is the priest. Every frame is rendered through the shot's own lens on a full-frame 16:9 sensor; the plan under it shows the camera from above (amber wedge = what the lens sees, dashed line = the camera move). The chapel is a stand-in until the real church is measured.
