Burning Church Storyboard
Director's previs storyboard — the church

BURNING

3D previs from the shot list in the director's bible. White figures without faces are the six dancers of the congregation; the dark figure is the priest. Every frame is rendered through the shot's own lens on a full-frame 16:9 sensor; the plan under it is the camera seen from above (wedge = what the lens sees, dashed line = the camera move). The church is modelled on the Sloterkerk in Sloten from the location photos (sizes estimated, not measured); the brass lectern stands in the middle as the priest's pulpit and a middle aisle is cleared through the chairs.
